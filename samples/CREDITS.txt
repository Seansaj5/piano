Woodshed piano samples

grand/    Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, recorded from a Yamaha C5 grand.
          License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
          Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3, taken from the MP3 mirror used by
          @tonejs/piano (https://github.com/tambien/Piano). Layer p = velocity layer 8, f = layer 14 (of 16).

upright/  Upright Piano KW by Gonzalo and Roberto (FreePats project), recorded from a Kawai upright.
          License: CC0 1.0 public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
          Source: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#UprightKW (2022-02-21 SFZ+FLAC).
          Layer p = the SFZ's soft zones (velocity 1-80), f = its loud zones (81-127).

Changes made for Woodshed: every file was trimmed to start at its attack, shortened with a fade-out (upright
zones that end early were extended through their own loop with a gentle decay), the upright bank was lowered
3 dB as a whole, three soft upright zones have the SFZ's low-pass filter baked in, and all files were re-encoded
as AAC (.m4a). File names are {midi of the sampled key}{layer}.m4a.

harpsichord/ celesta/ musicbox/ vibes/
          FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen, as rendered note by note (MP3) for MIDI.js by Benjamin Gleitzman,
          https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts. License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. One note every three
          semitones was taken; file names are {midi}.mp3.

trombone/ Tenor trombone sustains from VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios (Sam Gossner),
          https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE. License: CC0 1.0 public domain dedication. The medium (v2) layer,
          trimmed to the attack, cut to 7 s with a fade, re-encoded as AAC. The app loops a steady stretch of each note
          while a key is held. VSCO names middle C "C3"; file names here use MIDI numbers (C4 = 60).
